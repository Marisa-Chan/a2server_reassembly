Packet::field_0x5 -> from_player_id -- sender player id (mirrors to_player_id); NOT player_id to avoid shadowing PacketJoin::player_id

PacketInfo::field_0xe/field_e1..e4 -> param2/param2_b0..b3 -- generic second dword parameter; bytes carry main_sphere/face/color in the char-gen packet (0x48)
